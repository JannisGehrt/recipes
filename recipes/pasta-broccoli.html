Lemony broccoli pasta with halloumi or salmon

Prep 10 min Cook 15 min Protein ~45 g per portion Topper salmon, one plate

The broccoli cooks in the pasta pot for the last four minutes, and the sauce comes together back in that pot, which frees the pan for the halloumi at the very end — it goes rubbery if it waits. The sauce is just garlic oil, parmesan, crème fraîche and starchy pasta water — save that water before you drain. Halloumi on both plates, or swap one plate's halloumi for salmon.

Ingredients

Pot & pan

  • Short pasta (orecchiette, penne)250 g
  • Broccoli1 head, ~400 g
  • Halloumi250 g
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Olive oil3 tbsp + a splash for the salmon

Topper, one plate

  • Salmon fillet, skin on1, ~150 g

Sauce & seasoning

  • Parmesan, grated40 g
  • Crème fraîche100 g
  • Lemon1
  • Chilli flakesa good pinch
  • Salt & black pepperto taste

Prep no heat · do it whenever

Cook one pot, one pan

  1. Pasta into the boiling salted water. Set the timer to the time on the pack — it chimes once with 4 minutes left for the broccoli.

    Chimes at 4 min left

  2. Meanwhile: large frying pan, medium-low heat. 3 tbsp olive oil, garlic and chilli flakes. Gently — the garlic should go pale gold, not brown. Then heat off.

  3. When the pasta chimes at 4 minutes: broccoli florets into the pot with the pasta.

  4. Topper · one plateWhen the broccoli goes in: small pan, medium-high, splash of oil. Salmon in skin side down, press flat with a spatula for the first 30 seconds so the skin doesn't curl. Leave it alone until the colour has crept most of the way up the side — it chimes to flip for the last minute. Lift out skin side up so it stays crisp.

    Chimes to flip at 1 min left

  5. Scoop out a full mug of pasta water, then drain. Pasta and broccoli back into the pot, and scrape the garlic oil from the pan in after them.

  6. Pot on low. Crème fraîche, parmesan, lemon zest, half the lemon's juice and a good splash of pasta water. Toss hard until glossy and it coats the pasta. Salt, lots of pepper, more lemon if needed. Heat off, lid on, and keep the rest of the pasta water.

  7. Frying pan, medium-high heat, no oil needed. Halloumi in one layer until golden on both sides.

  8. Loosen the pasta with a splash of pasta water if it's thickened, then into bowls with the halloumi straight from the pan on top — or the salmon on one plate, skin up. Extra parmesan and chilli at the table.

Sauce clumpy or dry? More pasta water, a splash at a time, and keep tossing off the heat — the starch pulls it back together.
