Pork fried rice with peas and egg

Prep 15 min Cook 30 min Protein ~55 g per portion

One pan, one pot for the rice. The pork goes in the shared base, so this one isn't vegetarian. Spread the cooked rice out to steam off before it hits the pan — that's what keeps it from going mushy.

Ingredients

Pan

  • Rice (basmati or jasmine)150 g
  • Pork schnitzel or fillet300 g
  • Frozen peas250 g
  • Eggs4
  • Spring onions1 bunch (4)
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Gingerthumb-sized piece
  • Neutral oil3 tbsp + 1 tsp

For the pork

  • Soy sauce1 tbsp
  • Cornflour1 tsp

Sauce

  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Sesame oil1 tsp
  • Rice or apple vinegar1 tsp
  • Sugar½ tsp
  • Black pepperlots
  • Chilli oil or srirachaat the table

Prep no heat · do it whenever

Cook one pot, one pan

  1. Rice with 225 ml water and a pinch of salt. Bring to the boil, lid on, lowest heat.

  2. Spread the rice flat on a large plate to steam off.

  3. Large pan, very hot, 1 tbsp oil. Pork strips in one layer and leave them alone for 2 minutes, then toss for 1 more. Out onto a plate.

    Chimes to toss at 1 min left

  4. Splash more oil into the same pan. Eggs in, stir until just set — about a minute. Onto the plate with the pork.

  5. 1 tbsp oil. Spring onion whites, garlic and ginger for a minute, then the peas.

  6. Rice in, press it flat and let it catch. Turn it twice along the way.

  7. Pork and egg back in, sauce and spring onion greens over. Toss for a minute, then into bowls. Chilli oil or sriracha at the table.

Rice clumping or soggy? It went in too wet. Turn the heat up, spread it flat and leave it alone longer between turns.
