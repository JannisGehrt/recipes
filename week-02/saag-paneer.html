Week 2 · Dinner 1 of 2 · serves 2

Saag paneer with peas & basmati

Prep 15 min Cook 25 min Protein ~45 g per portion Topper chicken strips, one plate

The yoghurt goes in off the heat at the very end — that's what pushes the protein up, and it splits if it boils.

Ingredients

Pan

  • Paneer250 g
  • Frozen spinach450 g
  • Frozen peas150 g
  • Onion1
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Ginger3 cm
  • Green chilli optional1
  • Greek yoghurt150 g
  • Basmati150 g
  • Lemon½
  • Ghee or oil2 tbsp

Topper, one plate

  • Chicken150 g

Spices

  • Cumin seeds1 tsp
  • Ground coriander1 tsp
  • Turmeric½ tsp
  • Garam masala1 tsp
  • Coriander leaves optionalhandful

Prep no heat · do it whenever

Cook yoghurt last, heat lowest

Tap a timer to start it, tap again to pause. Running timers stay in the bar at the bottom. When one finishes, the page rings for 5 seconds and flashes — keep the page open and the screen on, a locked phone can't ring.

  1. Rice: 300 ml water, ½ tsp salt, lid on, boil, then the lowest heat. When it rings, off the hob, lid on until serving.

  2. Large pan, medium-high, ghee. Paneer in a single layer, turning once or twice, until golden. Lift out — leave the fat in the pan.

  3. Medium heat. Cumin seeds for 20 seconds, then the onion with a pinch of salt.

  4. Garlic, ginger and chilli, then the ground coriander and turmeric. Stir until toasty, not raw.

  5. Frozen spinach with 100 ml water, lid on, breaking it up once or twice as it thaws.

  6. Peas and garam masala in, uncovered, until most of the liquid has cooked off.

  7. Topper · one plateSmall pan, high heat, oil. Chicken strips until browned through.

  8. Heat to its lowest setting. Stir in the yoghurt, then the paneer. Lemon, salt properly. Serve on the rice with coriander.

Sauce split? The pan was too hot for the yoghurt. Off the hob, a splash of cold water, stir firmly.
