Week 2 · Dinner 2 of 2 · serves 2

Broccoli & white bean soup with cheese toasties

Prep 10 min Cook 30 min Protein ~40 g per portion Topper ham in the toastie, one plate

The beans give body without cream. The florets go in late so the soup stays green.

Ingredients

Soup

  • Broccoli1 large head
  • White beans1 × 400 g
  • Onion1
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • Vegetable stock700 ml
  • Crème fraîche100 g
  • Lemon½
  • Butter or oil1 tbsp
  • Nutmega grating

Topper, one toastie

  • Cooked hama few slices

Toasties

  • Cheddar or Bergkäse, grated180 g
  • Bread4 slices
  • Butterfor the outsides

Prep no heat · do it whenever

Cook blend before the cheese goes in

Tap a timer to start it, tap again to pause. Running timers stay in the bar at the bottom. When one finishes, the page plays a short chime (tap anywhere to stop it) — keep the page open and the screen on, a locked phone can't ring.

  1. Pot, medium heat, butter or oil. Onion with a pinch of salt until soft and translucent.

  2. Garlic in for a minute. Then the beans, the broccoli stalk and all the stock. Lid on, up to a simmer.

  3. Florets in, lid back on — bright green and just tender, no further.

  4. Off the heat. Blend smooth. Then, on the lowest heat, stir in 60 g of the cheese and the crème fraîche. Nutmeg, lemon, salt, lots of pepper.

  5. Topper · one toastieToasties: cheese between the slices, ham in one only, butter outside. Dry pan, medium, a smaller pan on top as a weight. The timer rings to flip.

    Flip alert at halfway

  6. Cut the toasties on the diagonal and serve alongside the bowls.

Stringy or grainy? The soup boiled after the cheese went in. Blend first, then keep it below a simmer.
