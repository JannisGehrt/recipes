Week 1 · Dinner 2 of 2 · serves 2

Halloumi & crispy chickpea bowl

Prep 15 min Cook 15 min + oven Protein ~50 g per portion Topper chicken breast, one plate

The oven does the chickpeas on its own. Fry the halloumi last and eat it immediately — it turns rubbery as it cools.

Ingredients

Bowl

  • Halloumi250 g
  • Chickpeas1 × 400 g
  • Couscous150 g
  • Cucumber1
  • Radishes1 bunch
  • Spring onions3
  • Parsley (and mint)big handful
  • Lemon1
  • Olive oil3 tbsp

Topper, one plate

  • Chicken breast1, ~150 g

Spices & yoghurt

  • Ground cumin1 tsp
  • Sweet paprika1 tsp
  • Greek yoghurt250 g
  • Harissa1–2 tsp
  • Saltto taste

Prep no heat · do it whenever

Cook the oven does most of it

Tap a timer to start it, tap again to pause. Running timers stay in the bar at the bottom. When one finishes, the page rings for 5 seconds and flashes — keep the page open and the screen on, a locked phone can't ring.

  1. Chickpea tray onto a high shelf. The timer rings halfway to shake the tray and turn it — without a fan the back of the oven runs hotter.

    Shake alert at halfway

  2. Couscous into a bowl with 200 ml boiling water, ½ tsp salt and a glug of olive oil. Plate over the top.

  3. Meanwhile: cucumber, radishes, spring onion and herbs into a big bowl with half the lemon's juice, 2 tbsp olive oil, salt and pepper.

  4. Harissa yoghurt: yoghurt, harissa, a squeeze of lemon, pinch of salt. Swirl — streaky beats fully mixed.

  5. When the couscous rings: fluff with a fork and fold into the salad. Taste for lemon and salt.

  6. Dry pan, medium-high, no oil. Halloumi in until deep golden; the timer rings to flip.

    Flip alert at halfway

  7. Topper · one plateSame pan, splash of oil. Chicken breast until cooked through, then slice.

    Flip alert at halfway

  8. Chickpeas out. Couscous salad in the bowls, halloumi on top, chickpeas over, harissa yoghurt across, lemon wedge on the side.

Chickpeas not crisp? They went on wet. Dry them in the towel until they look matte.
