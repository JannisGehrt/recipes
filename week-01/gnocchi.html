Week 1 · Dinner 1 of 2 · serves 2

Creamy white bean & spinach gnocchi

Prep 10 min Cook 25 min Protein ~44 g per portion Topper bacon or chorizo, one plate

The sauce is blended cottage cheese, not cream — that's where the protein comes from. It goes in at the end with the heat right down; boiling makes it grainy.

Ingredients

Pan

  • Gnocchi500 g
  • White beans1 × 400 g
  • Baby spinach200 g
  • Onion1
  • Garlic3 cloves
  • Olive oil2 tbsp

Topper, one plate

  • Bacon or chorizo100 g

Sauce & seasoning

  • Cottage cheese250 g
  • Hard cheese, grated50 g
  • Tomato paste1 tbsp
  • Dried thyme1 tsp
  • Chilli flakesa pinch
  • Nutmega grating
  • Lemon½

Prep no heat · do it whenever

Cook one pan

Tap a timer to start it, tap again to pause. Running timers stay in the bar at the bottom. When one finishes, the page plays a short chime (tap anywhere to stop it) — keep the page open and the screen on, a locked phone can't ring.

  1. Large pan, medium heat, 2 tbsp olive oil. Gnocchi straight from the pack in a single layer, and don't stir — you want brown, crusty patches. If the pan won't fit them in one layer, do two batches.

  2. Push the gnocchi to one side, onion and a pinch of salt into the space, stirring now and then.

  3. Garlic, thyme, chilli and tomato paste in. Stir until the paste darkens slightly and stops smelling raw.

  4. Beans in with 100 ml water and a pinch of salt. Lid on, heat to medium-low. Nothing to do until it rings.

  5. Lid off. Spinach in by the handful, stirring until it collapses.

  6. Heat to its lowest setting. Stir in the cottage cheese mixture until glossy. Nutmeg, half the lemon, salt properly. Splash of water if it's tight.

  7. Topper · one plateSmall pan, no oil, medium heat. Bacon or chorizo until the edges crisp. Spoon onto one plate.

  8. Serve straight from the pan with pepper and a little more cheese.

Split or grainy? The heat was too high. Off the hob, a splash of cold water, stir hard — it usually comes back.
