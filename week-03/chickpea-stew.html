Chickpea & spinach stew with fried bread

Prep 10 min Cook 30 min Protein ~35 g per portion Topper chorizo, one plate

Better the next day — cook this one if you want a lunch out of it.

Ingredients

Stew

  • Chickpeas2 × 400 g
  • Onion1
  • Garlic4 cloves
  • Rustic bread2 thick slices
  • Baby spinach200 g
  • Vegetable stock300 ml
  • Olive oil4 tbsp+
  • Eggs2

Topper, one plate

  • Chorizo100 g

Seasoning

  • Tomato paste2 tbsp
  • Smoked paprika1 tbsp
  • Ground cumin1 tsp
  • Cayennea pinch
  • Sherry or red wine vinegar1 tbsp
  • Saltto taste

Prep no heat · do it whenever

Cook the bread is the thickener

Tap a timer to start it, tap again to pause. Running timers stay in the bar at the bottom. When one finishes, the page plays a short chime (tap anywhere to stop it) — keep the page open and the screen on, a locked phone can't ring.

  1. Wide pan, medium-high, 4 tbsp olive oil — more than feels right. Bread chunks and all the garlic in, turning, until the bread is golden and the garlic soft. Lift both into a bowl and leave the oil behind.

  2. Crush the bread and garlic to a rough paste with a fork. Stir in the vinegar and a splash of water. Set aside — this is what thickens the stew, so don't skip it.

  3. Onion into the same pan with a pinch of salt, medium heat, until soft and golden at the edges.

  4. Tomato paste, smoked paprika, cumin and cayenne in. Keep stirring — paprika burns and turns bitter fast.

  5. Chickpeas and stock in. Bring to a simmer and let it reduce, uncovered, to a loose and saucy consistency rather than a soup.

  6. Stir in the bread paste — it thickens almost straight away. Then the spinach a handful at a time until wilted. Salt properly and taste: it should be slightly sharp.

  7. Topper · one plateDry pan, medium heat. Chorizo until the edges crisp and the red fat runs. Spoon over one bowl.

  8. Fry the eggs in a little olive oil until the whites are set and the edges lacy. One on each bowl.

Tastes flat? It needs vinegar, not salt. Add another teaspoon and taste again.
