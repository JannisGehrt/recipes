Sesame-peanut noodles with jammy eggs

Prep 10 min Cook 15 min Protein ~40 g per portion Topper prawns, one plate

Ingredients

Bowl

  • Egg noodles or soba180 g
  • Eggs4
  • Frozen edamame200 g
  • Carrot1
  • Cucumber½
  • Spring onions3
  • Coriander optionalhandful
  • Sesame seedsto finish

Topper, one plate

  • Prawns150 g

Sauce

  • Peanut butter4 tbsp
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Rice vinegar or lemon juice1 tbsp
  • Honey1 tbsp
  • Sesame oil1 tsp
  • Sambal oelek1–2 tsp
  • Garlic, grated1 clove
  • Ginger, grated3 cm
  • Warm water4–6 tbsp

Prep no heat · do it whenever

Cook one pot of water does it all

Tap a timer to start it, tap again to pause. Running timers stay in the bar at the bottom. When one finishes, the page plays a short chime (tap anywhere to stop it) — keep the page open and the screen on, a locked phone can't ring.

  1. Big pot of water to a rolling boil. Lower the eggs in on a spoon and start the timer as the last one goes in — 6½ minutes gives jammy yolks.

  2. Eggs out into a bowl of cold water. Leave the pot boiling. Peel them once they're cool enough to hold.

  3. Noodles into the same water. Set the timer to whatever the pack says. Frozen edamame go in for the last 3 minutes — the timer rings when.

    Edamame alert at 3:00 left

  4. Drain both together and rinse under cold water. It stops the cooking and washes off the starch that would glue the noodles into a block.

  5. Into a big bowl with the sauce, carrot, cucumber and most of the spring onion. Toss with tongs or your hands until everything is coated. Taste — it usually wants more vinegar or salt.

  6. Topper · one plateHot pan, a drop of oil. Prawns 2 minutes a side until pink all over.

    Flip alert at halfway

  7. Halve the eggs and sit them on top. Sesame seeds, the rest of the spring onion, coriander. Eat straight away — the sauce stiffens as it cools.

Sauce won't come together? Keep adding warm water a tablespoon at a time and keep whisking. Natural peanut butter needs more water than the sweetened kind.
