Mushroom & quark stroganoff with rice

Prep 15 min Cook 25 min Protein ~35 g per portion Topper beef strips, one plate

Ingredients

Main

  • Brown mushrooms400 g
  • Onion1
  • Garlic2 cloves
  • White beans1 × 400 g
  • Magerquark250 g
  • Gherkins2
  • Dill or parsley1 bunch
  • Rice150 g

Topper, one plate

  • Beef strips150 g

Sauce

  • Tomato paste1 tbsp
  • Dijon mustard1 tbsp
  • Sweet paprika1 tsp
  • Worcestershire sauce1 tbsp
  • Vegetable stock200 ml
  • Cornflour1 tbsp
  • Oil2 tbsp

Prep no heat · do it whenever

Cook the quark goes in last, off the heat

Tap a timer to start it, tap again to pause. Running timers stay in the bar at the bottom. When one finishes, the page plays a short chime (tap anywhere to stop it) — keep the page open and the screen on, a locked phone can't ring.

  1. Rice: 300 ml water and ½ tsp salt, lid on, bring to the boil, then down to the lowest heat. When the timer goes, take it off the hob and leave the lid on until you serve.

  2. Large pan, high heat, 2 tbsp oil. Mushrooms in and leave them alone — the timer rings when you can stir. They give out water, it boils off, then they brown. Browning is the dish.

    Stir alert at halfway

  3. Onion in, heat down to medium, until soft.

  4. Garlic, tomato paste, paprika and mustard in. Stir for a minute.

  5. Stock, Worcestershire and the beans in. Let it simmer; the timer rings when it's time to stir in the cornflour slurry.

    Slurry alert at 1:00 left

  6. Topper · one plateSmall pan, very hot, oil. Beef strips, barely moving them, until browned outside. Rest on a plate.

  7. Off the heat entirely. Stir in the quark, then the gherkins. Taste and salt. If it needs more lift, a splash of the gherkin jar liquid.

  8. Serve on the rice with lots of dill or parsley, and the beef over one portion.

Sauce gone grainy? The pan was still too hot for the quark. Off the hob, a splash of cold water, stir hard — it comes back.
